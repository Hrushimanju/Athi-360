# Hrushimanju

**Status:** In development

## The problem

Most people own clothes that do not suit their colouring and have no affordable way to find out what does.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants to dress well from the wardrobe they already own.

## What it does today

In development: AI-guided personal colour analysis applied to the clothes you already own. Research and legal groundwork are underway; no public site yet.

## What it could mean later

Knowing what to wear without hiring a colourist.
