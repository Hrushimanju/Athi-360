# Astral Nexus

**Status:** Live

## The problem

World cultures built deep mathematical and symbolic traditions to read the sky, the palm and dreams—yet modern astrology apps scatter and flatten them into generic, ad-ridden pop horoscopes that never show how different traditions interpret the same blueprint.

## Who it is for

Curious explorers and pattern-seekers who want to enter their birth details, palm readings or dream symbols and see how different cultural systems and traditions interpret them side by side, alongside scholars working with historical manuscripts.

## What it does today

A live multi-tradition platform at astralnexus.athi360.uk across 7 languages: calculates your birth chart (Lagna, Moon sign, rising sign, nakshatras and dashas) on your own device with a real open-source ephemeris, alongside cross-cultural reading traditions, card decks and dream dossiers backed by 530+ researched sources.

## What it could mean later

Expanded palmistry (Hastarekha) and comparative cultural reading modules, place-aware sky tools, and a cited archive of ancient palm leaves and historical texts.
