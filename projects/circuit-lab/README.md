# Circuit Lab 360

**Status:** Live

## The problem

Standard electronics teaching only tells you that a circuit fails or calls a method wrong—no one teaches you why it fails unless you physically experiment. For small equipment trial-and-error helps, but for big applications you are too afraid of costly mistakes to experiment freely, while innovators wanting to upgrade a specific electrical component lack a clean simulation environment.

## Who it is for

Innovators with brilliant ideas to upgrade specific electrical components, complete beginners and students who want electronics taught in the simplest way with clear explanations of why circuits fail, and researchers or engineers planning the placement of electrical components inside confined systems.

## What it does today

A live parametric CAD and SPICE simulation studio at circuit-lab.athi360.uk powered by real ngspice WebAssembly in the browser, with 24 verified packs, AC Bode sweeps, 94 automated tests, and a 6-variant C9 ablation tutor that shows exactly where and why a circuit fails so you can focus on brainstorming ideas.

## What it could mean later

Turning small electrical theories into big innovations: simulating component upgrades and where they can be applied, planning component placement inside confined systems for researchers, and finding leaner, higher-efficiency circuits verified by a real SPICE solver.
