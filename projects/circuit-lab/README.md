# Circuit Lab 360

**Status:** In design

## The problem

Electronics feels closed to people who know nothing about it: the tools assume you already understand the maths.

## Who it is for

Complete beginners, students, and curious tinkerers.

## What it does today

In design. The specification is written: a circuit sandbox where you place and wire parts and a tutor explains in plain language what is happening and warns before a part fails. No public build yet.

## What it could mean later

A guided path from first circuit to board-level thinking, built on a real circuit simulator.
