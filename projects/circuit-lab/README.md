# Circuit Lab 360

**Status:** Live

## The problem

Standard electronics teaching tells you that a circuit failed without showing why unless you damage real parts. On expensive equipment the risk discourages experimentation, while makers testing component changes and researchers planning layouts inside confined enclosures lack a verifiable browser lab.

## Who it is for

Makers and engineers testing upgrades to specific electrical components, beginners and students who want clear explanations of why circuits fail, and researchers planning component placement inside confined systems.

## What it does today

A live parametric CAD and SPICE simulation studio at circuit-lab.athi360.uk powered by real ngspice WebAssembly in the browser, with 24 verified packs, AC Bode sweeps, 103 engine solver tests and 1,197 unit tests, a 6-variant C9 ablation engine, and a live AI Explain, Fix, and Tutor drawer on Azure AI Foundry (DeepSeek-V4) behind a global daily spend cap.

## What it could mean later

Simulating component upgrades and where they perform best, planning component placement inside confined systems for researchers, and finding leaner, higher-efficiency circuits verified by a real SPICE solver.
