# ConsumerSuit

**Status:** Live

## The problem

Ordinary people facing a dispute cannot afford a solicitor and do not know how the law applies to them, while legal departments, advice bodies, courts and tribunals face severe case backlogs that delay justice for months or years.

## Who it is for

Everyday citizens (UK tenants, consumers and employees, including people whose first language is not English) preparing to fight their own case, alongside legal departments, advice agencies and tribunal judges working to clear case backlogs.

## What it does today

A live self-help web app (not legal advice) that helps the common person understand the relevant law across 28 areas of England and Wales civil and employment law, prepares them to fight their own case, tracks critical deadlines, and structures the full chronology and context so legal department and tribunal backlogs can be reduced dramatically.

## What it could mean later

Clearing legal and tribunal backlogs fast: giving citizens rapid self-help legal preparation and giving tribunal judges clear, structured case context to speed up hearings and final judgments.
