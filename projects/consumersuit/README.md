# ConsumerSuit

**Status:** Live

## The problem

Ordinary people facing a consumer, tenancy, or workplace dispute often cannot afford a solicitor and struggle to work out which statute sections or time limits apply to their facts before critical deadlines expire.

## Who it is for

Everyday citizens in England and Wales (tenants, consumers, and employees, including people whose first language is not English) organising their own dispute timeline, evidence, and correspondence as litigants in person.

## What it does today

A live self-help legal information web app in public beta (not legal advice and not a law firm) covering 63 statutes across 8 areas of England and Wales civil and employment law. It structures scattered notes and documents into a chronological timeline, checks statutory deadlines, fetches live statute sections from legislation.gov.uk, and publishes open accuracy scorecards at consumer-suit.uk/beta.html.

## What it could mean later

Solicitor-reviewed pilot evaluations with university law clinics and advice bodies, tighter multi-run section determinism, and structured summaries designed to help advisers and tribunal panels read litigant-in-person bundles faster.
