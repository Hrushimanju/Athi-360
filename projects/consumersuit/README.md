# ConsumerSuit

**Status:** Live

## The problem

Tenants, consumers and employees with a genuine claim often cannot afford help and do not know where to start.

## Who it is for

UK tenants, consumers and employees.

## What it does today

A live web app that helps you set out your UK rights case clearly and in order, with the relevant law explained along the way.

## What it could mean later

More case types and clearer guidance at every step.
